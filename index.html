Strength training for first-timers

Never set foot in a gym? Coming back after a long break? Extreme Fitness Gym is small, friendly and built for beginners. A coach is on the floor at every session, so you are never left guessing.

New to the gym? Here's how we help

  • Free induction

    Your first visit is a free 30-minute session with a coach. We show you the equipment and build a simple plan around your goals.

  • Small classes

    Classes are capped at 10 people, so the coach can check your technique and adjust exercises for your level.

  • A coach on the floor

    Whenever the gym is open, a qualified coach is available to answer questions. Just ask.
