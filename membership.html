Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No. Most of our members started with no gym experience. Your coach will choose exercises that suit your current fitness and build up from there.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Comfortable clothes, indoor shoes, a water bottle and a towel. Lockers are free, so bring a padlock if you have one.

Is there a contract?

No. Weekly plans run until you cancel. To cancel, tell us at the front desk or by email at least two weeks before your next payment.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. You can pause for up to eight weeks a year, for example for travel or illness, at no cost.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can switch plans at any time, and the new price starts from your next payment.

Not sure which plan suits you?

Book a free trial. After your induction, your coach can help you choose.
